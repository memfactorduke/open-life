# Mem's Life vocabulary

Shared vocabulary for tasks, workouts, and food records. These definitions name domain concepts; they do not prescribe a storage schema.

## Language

**Board**:
A configurable workflow that organizes tasks into columns.

**Project**:
A grouping of related tasks contributing to a larger purpose.

**Column**:
A named stage within a board's workflow.

**Card**:
A task's visual representation on a board, not a separate copy of its underlying work.

**Task**:
An outcome to achieve. It can be simple or contain subtasks performed by a person, agents, or both.

**Subtask**:
An actionable part of a parent task, with its own responsibility and completion criteria. A subtask is presented as a step in the parent's workflow.

**Dependency**:
A prerequisite that must be satisfied before a subtask can begin.

**Task view**:
A filtered presentation of existing tasks or subtasks, such as work needing the person or work ready for an agent. A view does not create new copies of the work.

**Release**:
The person's deliberate choice to make work available for execution. Satisfying a dependency does not release an otherwise planned task.

**Skill binding**:
The reusable agent procedure selected for a subtask, together with the inputs and expected result for that use.

**Agent run**:
A particular attempt by an agent to carry out a task or subtask. A retry is another run on the same work.

**Handoff**:
A transfer of responsibility or a request for the next participant's input while preserving the task and its history.

**Planned**:
Work recorded for possible future action, but not yet selected for execution.

**Next**:
Work deliberately selected to happen next. Selection is separate from whether a particular subtask's prerequisites are satisfied.

**Active**:
Work that has begun. It can remain active while waiting for a person or an agent.

**Watcher**:
An external process that discovers eligible agent work and coordinates its execution through the app's MCP interface.

**Approval**:
A person's explicit acceptance of a particular result version. Viewing a result alone does not establish approval.

**Workout plan**:
The intended training activities and targets for upcoming workouts. Revisions affect plans, not the record of what actually happened.

**Routine**:
A reusable workout plan containing an ordered collection of planned exercises and their targets.

**Exercise**:
A named activity with suitable ways to record performance, such as repetitions and weight, duration, or distance.

**Workout session**:
An actual workout, including its performed exercises, recorded results, and timing. Its record is separate from the editable plan it may have followed.

**Set result**:
Recorded performance for one exercise set, such as repetitions and weight or duration and distance as appropriate to the activity.

**Workout note**:
Free text attached to a workout session or an exercise within it, describing what happened or information useful next time.

**Provider observation**:
A record supplied by an external service, with its source and observation time preserved. WHOOP observations may supplement a local workout session without replacing the person's recorded sets or notes.

**Food entry**:
A dated record of food consumed, initially represented by photos and a written description. It is separate from a future meal plan or calculated nutrition data.
