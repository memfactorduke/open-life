# Release progress

Mem's Life is implemented. On October 13, 2026, **28 of 29 tickets and 153 of 154 acceptance criteria are verified**. Ticket 29 remains open for physical-camera and live-provider validation. The source is in [PR #2](https://github.com/memfactorduke/open-life/pull/2).

## Built product

The web and native clients provide Today, configurable task boards, recurring work, projects, mixed human/agent steps, reusable skill bindings, claims, progress, versioned deliverables and approval. Scoped generic MCP clients use the same PostgreSQL operations as the owner interfaces. External agents execute the work.

Workout tracking covers exercise catalogs, four exercise modes, routines, supersets, targets, performed sets, rest/exercise timers, notes, session changes, corrections and comparable history. Editable plans remain separate from recorded activity. Food supports private text and photos with durable native capture and recovery. Nutrition and meal planning remain later scope. WHOOP's official read-only adapter is implemented and fixture-tested; no personal account is connected.
